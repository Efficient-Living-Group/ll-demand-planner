# Demand Planner Design Context

## Target audience
Lifely founders and operations leaders who need a portfolio-wide read before opening an individual Category Killer.

## Primary use cases
- Answer in under 30 seconds: what needs attention now, where stock risk sits, and what is happening in the PO pipeline.
- Compare Category Killer health without opening every tab.
- Drill from a portfolio signal directly into the relevant Category Killer or PO view.
- Confirm data freshness before making a decision.

## Brand personality and tone
Calm, commercially direct, operational, trustworthy. Use Lifely's existing deep teal, soft green-tinted neutrals, and restrained semantic red/amber/green. Avoid decorative dashboard theatrics.

## Design principles
- Prioritize decisions over raw data.
- Show four headline signals, then a short action queue, then compact supporting detail.
- Treat red as an exception, not decoration.
- Use spacing and dividers before adding more cards.
- Keep summaries explicit about units and definitions; do not imply customer-order counts when the data is component demand.
- Every alert must drill into an existing detailed view.
- Desktop should support a fast portfolio scan; mobile should preserve the action queue and turn dense rows into stacked summaries.
